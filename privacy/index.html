Your data, plainly

What the Aoze app and this website do, and don't do, with your data. Everything below was checked against Aoze's source code.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short

  • Aoze processes your images, GIFs and clips on your PC. They are not sent anywhere.
  • The only connection the app makes on its own is the update check on GitHub, which you can turn off.
  • The website measures its own audience, with no third-party service and no ads. It doesn't store your IP address, and you can opt out in one click.
  • The website is hosted in France by o2switch and the downloads by GitHub: like any host, each sees your IP address.

Who is responsible

Aoze and this website are published by Zero (see the legal notice). For any question about your data: contact@aoze.fr.

The Aoze app

Your files stay on your PC

The images, GIFs and clips you open are processed on your computer, by programs installed with Aoze (waifu2x, FFmpeg, RIFE, Real-ESRGAN). Aoze sends no file, no image and no file name over the Internet. There is no account, no telemetry, no automatic crash report and no advertising.

What Aoze stores on your PC

Settings

Your preferences (language, presets, output folder…). In %APPDATA%\app.aoze.

History

For each processed file: its path, the result's path, dimensions, sizes and the settings used. You can clear it from the History screen. In %APPDATA%\app.aoze.

Performance measurements

Speeds measured on your hardware, used to tune the settings. In %APPDATA%\app.aoze.

Log

An aoze.log file (2 MB at most) that may contain file paths and your graphics card's name. In %LOCALAPPDATA%\app.aoze\logs.

Cache

Previews, temporary work files, pasted images and downloaded updates. In %LOCALAPPDATA%\app.aoze.

These files never leave your PC. When you uninstall, tick the option to delete the app's data to remove them all.

Diagnostic report

If you click the button that copies the diagnostics, Aoze puts a technical report (versions, graphics card, end of the log) on your clipboard. Nothing is sent: you decide whether to share it. It may contain folder paths that include your Windows user name, so read it before posting it.

Windows components

Aoze draws its interface with Microsoft Edge WebView2, a Windows component. If your PC doesn't have it, the installer downloads it from Microsoft. That component follows Microsoft's rules. Links (GitHub, Ko-fi) open in your browser.

Updates

When Aoze opens, if Check for updates is on (it is by default), the app reads the latest.json file of the latest release on GitHub. If there is a new version, it offers it to you; it only downloads it from GitHub Releases when you ask.

As with any connection, GitHub receives the technical data of the request (IP address, date, file requested). Aoze adds no file, no identifier and no usage data to it. You can turn this option off in Settings, under Updates.

The website

This website is made of static pages, with no forms, no accounts and no ads. Fonts and images are served by the site itself (no Google Fonts, no video embedded from another site).

Hosting

The website is hosted in France by o2switch. Like any host, o2switch records the technical data of each visit (IP address, browser, page requested, date) in the server logs, to serve the site and protect it. We don't use them to follow visitors or to measure the audience.

Version and download count

The home and download pages ask GitHub's API (api.github.com) for the latest version number and the total number of downloads, straight from your browser. GitHub therefore receives your IP address and information about your browser. No cookie is used for this.

Downloading

The Download button goes through aoze.fr/get/, which notes the request (see audience measurement below) and then sends you to the file hosted on GitHub Releases. GitHub also counts the downloads of each file. We don't know whether the download finished, or who made it.

Audience measurement

To know which pages are useful, where visitors come from and whether the download is easy to find, the website counts its visits itself, on our server at o2switch. No third-party analytics, no advertising, no tracking from one site to another: these statistics are only used to improve this website, are neither sold nor shared, and are not combined with other data.

What is noted

The visit

The pages viewed, the entry and exit pages, how long each page is on screen, the language of the site.

Where it comes from

The name of the site you came from (for example discord.com, without the rest of the address), and the utm_ campaign parameters of a shared link.

The device

Computer, phone or tablet, and the name of the browser and the system, read from the header every browser sends. No precise version, no browser fingerprint.

The country

Worked out from your IP address with a database installed on our server (DB-IP's IP to Country Lite, CC BY 4.0 licence). The IP address itself is not stored.

The download

Whether the Download button was seen and clicked, whether the Windows warning was accepted and whether the installer was requested, with its version.

To recognise a visit that goes on or a visitor who comes back, the website sets an aoze_v cookie: a random identifier that says nothing about you (see cookies below). Against abuse, an encrypted fingerprint of the IP address counts requests per minute; it is erased within the hour.

Nothing is measured when your browser sends the Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, nor for bots.

Opting out

This choice applies to this browser and is kept for 13 months.

Cookies and local storage

The website uses no advertising pixel, no tracker from another site and no third-party cookie. It may set two cookies of its own, for aoze.fr only:

  • aoze_v: the random identifier used for audience measurement. It lasts 13 months from the first visit and is never extended. Only the website's server can read it.
  • aoze_optout: only if you opt out, to remember that choice for 13 months.

This measurement cookie is only used for this website's statistics and meets the conditions of the French data protection authority (CNIL) to be exempt from consent: that is why there is no banner. You can still refuse it above, or clear this site's cookies in your browser.

A value may also be saved in local storage: aoze-lang, when you switch language with the EN / FR button. It only remembers that choice and is never sent anywhere.

Third-party services

  • GitHub hosts the code and the downloads, and answers the update check. GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Ko-fi receives voluntary donations. The website only links to ko-fi.com: no Ko-fi script is loaded here, and payment happens entirely on Ko-fi and its payment providers. We never see your payment details. Ko-fi's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft provides WebView2, which the app uses to draw its interface.
  • DB-IP publishes the database that gives the country of an IP address. We download it to our server every month: no address is sent to them.

When you open one of these services, its own rules apply.

If you write to us

If you send us an email, we receive your address and your message, and use them only to answer you. GitHub issues are public and follow GitHub's rules.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Answering your messages: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), so we can reply to people who write to us.
  • Hosting the website at o2switch and distributing Aoze through GitHub: legitimate interest, to publish the website and distribute Aoze. o2switch hosts the website for us; GitHub processes the technical data of its servers (IP address, logs) as an independent controller, under its own privacy statement.
  • Checking for updates: legitimate interest, to offer you fixes and new versions. The request goes straight to GitHub, we receive nothing from it, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Measuring the website's audience: legitimate interest, to understand how the website is used and improve it. The measurement cookie is exempt from consent (Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act) because it is limited to this measurement; you can object at any time.

How long data is kept

  • Your emails: 3 years after the last exchange, then they are deleted.
  • Audience measurement: the details of visits and download requests for 6 months, then only daily totals, without any identifier, for 25 months at most. The aoze_v cookie lasts 13 months.
  • GitHub's logs: as set out in GitHub's statement. We don't keep them and have no access to them.
  • The app's data: on your PC, until you delete it.

Transfers outside the European Union

GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States, so the technical data of the requests made to GitHub (version shown on the site, downloads, update check) may be processed there. The website itself is hosted in France. GitHub is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which covers these transfers.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (the GDPR in particular), you can ask to access your data, correct it, erase it, restrict its use or take a copy of it, or object to its use. Write to contact@aoze.fr.

Audience measurements are only linked to the cookie's random identifier: we cannot connect them to your name. Opting out deletes that cookie. A request about GitHub's logs or a Ko-fi payment often has to go to those services. You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country, in France the CNIL.

Changes

If Aoze or the website change how they handle data, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change. The history of this page is public in the GitHub repository.
